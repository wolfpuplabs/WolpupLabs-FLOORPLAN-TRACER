Quick Guide

  • Free Draw: Start drawing right away! Each grid box = 1 meter.
  • Wall: Tap 1x start point, tap other pos. Tap 2x (or ESC) to finish the wall line.
  • Edit & Delete Wall: Use 'Select/Edit' mode, click any wall line to drag it around or delete it.
  • Floors: Add / copy levels in the "Floors" panel. The active floor is the one you draw on; the others show as a ghost in 3D and a dashed underlay in 2D.
  • Walk (POV): Press Walk above the 3D view to stand inside the plan. Drag to look, use the joystick or WASD to move, and stairs are climbed automatically — so you can walk up to the next floor.
  • Void / Opening: Stairs cut their own opening in the floor above. For any other hole — a double-height space or a mezzanine — use the Void tool: activate the floor that should have the hole, drop a void on it, then set its Width and Depth.
  • Wall Transform (3D): Click a wall in the 3D view to grab it with the gizmo — Move, Rotate, and Scale (X = length, Y = height, Z = thickness), or type exact numbers in Properties.
